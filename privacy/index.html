2. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data is processed when you use the resvp iOS application, the website at getresvp.app, and related support services.

resvp is a location-based music discovery and social application. Users can share music and moods connected with their current area, discover nearby activity, interact with other users, and exchange messages.

3. Categories of personal data

Depending on how you use resvp, we may process the following categories of personal data:

a. Account and authentication data

This may include your user ID, email address, authentication provider, authentication tokens or identifiers, account creation date, and account status.

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide an identifier, your name, and either your email address or an Apple private relay address, depending on the choices you make with Apple.

Passwords are handled through the authentication provider and are not stored by us in readable form.

b. Profile data

This may include your username, display name, profile image, biography, and other information you choose to make available through your profile.

Public profile information may be visible to other resvp users.

c. Music and activity data

This may include songs, artists, artwork references, streaming links, moods, timestamps, likes, resonates, comments, replies, and other interactions with content.

d. Location data

When you grant location permission, resvp may process your device location to provide nearby music discovery and location-related sharing.

resvp is designed to associate shared content with an approximate geographic zone rather than publicly displaying your precise coordinates. Location zones are currently based on areas of approximately 750 metres. Depending on your position within a zone and the information shown in the app, other users may still be able to draw approximate conclusions about your location.

You should only share when you are comfortable revealing your approximate area. You can disable location access at any time in your iOS settings, although location-dependent features may then be unavailable.

e. Communications and user-generated content

This may include direct messages, song messages, comments, replies, reports, support messages, and other content you submit.

Direct messages are intended for the participants in the relevant conversation. They are not end-to-end encrypted. They may be stored and processed by our infrastructure and may be accessed when reasonably necessary for security, support, abuse prevention, enforcement of our Terms, or compliance with legal obligations.

f. Subscription and transaction data

When you purchase or restore a subscription through Apple, we may receive transaction and entitlement information such as product identifier, subscription status, purchase or renewal date, expiration date, trial or offer status, and an app-specific purchaser identifier.

We do not receive or store your complete payment-card information. Payments are processed by Apple.

g. Technical and security data

This may include device and operating-system information, app version, language, time zone, IP address, request timestamps, crash or diagnostic information, security events, and information required to prevent misuse and operate the service.

h. Website and support data

When you visit getresvp.app, hosting providers may process technical request data such as IP address, date and time, requested page, referrer information, browser information, and server logs.

If you contact us, we process your contact details, message, and any information you provide to respond to your request.

Where processing is based on legitimate interests, those interests include maintaining a secure and functional service, understanding technical performance, preventing misuse, protecting legal rights, and responding to users.

Please do not publish personal or sensitive information that you do not want others to see.

Blocking another user limits interactions within the functionality provided by resvp, but it cannot guarantee that previously shared or externally copied information will disappear.

Content involving other users may require limited residual records to preserve conversation integrity, enforce safety measures, or document transactions. Deleted messages may be represented by a neutral deletion notice rather than disappearing without a trace from the conversation.
